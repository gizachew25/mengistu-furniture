Our story

Quality furniture, made in Yeduha

Mengistu Furniture is a furniture workshop based in Yeduha Town, East Gojam, in the Amhara Region of Ethiopia. We craft quality ready-made furniture and build custom pieces to our customers’ exact designs.

From sofas, beds and dining sets to bufies, wardrobes and doors, every piece is built by hand with care, using solid materials and a keen eye for detail. Our goal is simple: stylish homes and better living for every family we serve.
